Open Industries: Temperate - Changelog

0.4.3 - 2026-07-31
- Corrected mirrored pixel-to-map-tile ownership in all 15 custom sites.
- Aligned artwork to the real four-by-four footprint and 64-pixel tile bounds.
- Fixed camera-movement fragments and neighbouring trees cutting through art.
- Restored canonical base-set icons for retained vanilla cargoes and Food.
- Added pixel-level ownership, footprint, and base-icon regression checks.

0.4.2 - 2026-07-30
- Split every custom industry into 16 camera-safe tile sprites.
- Preserved all published tile IDs and upgraded existing 0.4.x sites in place.
- Integrated supplied Food Market, Department Store, and Office Building art.
- Renamed Newsagent / Office to Office Building without changing house ID 2.
- Added tile reconstruction and town-building artwork validation.

0.4.1 - 2026-07-30
- Redrew all 15 custom industries in a restrained base-game-style 8bpp look.
- Removed the raised square terrain plates from custom sites.
- Added deterministic crop, scale, hard-alpha, and palette conversion.
- Added full four-by-four sorting bounds to prevent scenery cutting through sites.
- Verified the final art in an OpenTTD 15.3 fixed-seed full-map render.

0.4.0 - 2026-07-30
- Assigned complete vanilla layouts to all 13 original industries.
- Added the 15 supplied custom industry designs as four-by-four 8bpp sites.
- Added safe OpenTTD palette conversion and transparent sprite handling.
- Corrected composite draw order so ground tiles cannot cover the artwork.
- Reserved stable custom tile IDs 113-128 and verified a fixed-seed visual map.

0.3.1 - 2026-07-30
- Restored original layouts and base-set graphics for replaced Temperate industries.
- Rebuilt new sites from compatible original industry tiles.
- Remapped 0.3.0 tile IDs so existing saves lose the oversized grey prototype art.
- Corrected custom sprite sorting footprints from 48x48 to 16x16.
- Verified OpenTTD 15.3 new-game, reload, 0.3.0 upgrade, and graphical render tests.

0.3.0 - 2026-07-30

- Added a complete German translation for the whole NewGRF interface.
- Added short and long quantity strings for all cargoes, fixing undefined text.
- Replaced generic industry boxes with 28 distinct 8bpp isometric designs.
- Redesigned all three cargo-accepting town buildings.
- Preserved cargo-reactive states and short equipment animations.
- Added automated translation and sprite quality checks.

0.2.1 - 2026-07-29

- Added direct vanilla Goods and Temperate-chain monetary benchmarks.
- Added completed-tree, rewarded-recipe, and primary-supply progression checks.
- Rebalanced Food, Building Materials, Vehicles, and Fuel so completed industry
  trees provide a noticeable but bounded reward.
- Preserved all public IDs, recipes, and save compatibility.

0.2.0 - 2026-07-29

- Added cargo-reactive idle, stockpiled-input, waiting-output, and high-output
  industry graphics.
- Added service-responsive production to seven primary industries, ranging
  from 50% to 200%.
- Added a neutral service band and gradual monthly production reviews.
- Added live production, service, recipe, stockpile, bonus, and pickup status.
- Added translated zone and role badges.
- Added explicit magic-bulldozer removal costs.
- Added optional automatic town-encroachment clearance that protects the final
  instance of each rural primary industry.
- Preserved save compatibility with version 0.1.0.

0.1.0 - 2026-07-29

- Initial OIT1 Temperate replacement economy.
- Added 28 cargoes, 28 industries, and three town-building destinations.
- Added 14 priority-ordered stockpiled processing recipes.
- Added optional Engineering Supplies, Fuel, Lubricants, Leather, and material
  production bonuses.
- Added population-scaled urban Waste production.
- Added original indexed 8bpp cargo and industry graphics.
- Added monetary simulation, validation, packaging, and OpenTTD smoke tests.
